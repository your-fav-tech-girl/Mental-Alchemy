Common questions

Before you get started

Is what I share actually private?

Your information is protected with encrypted connections and access controls. Before handling protected health information in production, complete the required security, legal, vendor, and compliance review.

What if my first therapist isn't the right fit?

That's common, and it's fine. You can switch therapists at any point, for any reason, at no extra cost we'll help you find a better match right away.

Do you offer in-person sessions, or only video?

Both. During matching, you can filter for therapists who offer in-person sessions near you, video-only, or a mix of the two.

What if I need help outside of business hours?

Mental Alchemy isn't a crisis service. If you're in immediate danger or crisis, call or text 988 (US) any time, day or night, or go to your nearest emergency room.
